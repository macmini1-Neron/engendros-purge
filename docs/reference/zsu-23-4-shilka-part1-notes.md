# ZSU-23-4 «Shilka» Part 1 — reference notes (driver's compartment & layout)

Source: **AFV INTERIORS Web Magazine, "Soviet ZSU-23-4 Shilka, Part 1"** (© 2002, 2003) — photos by
David S. Speaks at the Threat Training Facility (TTF), 547th Intelligence Squadron, Nellis AFB.
PDF saved alongside this file: `zsu-23-4-shilka-part1-afv-interiors.pdf`. (Parts 2 & 3 cover the
turret + crew assignments — not in this PDF.)

Captured because it directly confirms/feeds the **named-rig** work (esp. the driver hatch we built).

## General
- Anti-aircraft replacement for the ZSU-57-2; Soviet's first radar-directed, vehicle-mounted AAA gun
  system. First public appearance 1965. Built on a newly designed **GM-575** chassis using PT-76
  suspension components.
- Crew up in the turret: **commander, gunner, range-finder**. **Driver** sits isolated in the
  **front-LEFT** hull (matches our rig: driver hatch on the front-left).
- Powered by a **V-6R 6-cylinder in-line diesel, mounted transversely**, **manual 5-speed + reverse**.
  Mechanical steering via **steering levers** (tiller bars); **torsion-bar** suspension.

## Driver's hatch — confirms our `hatch_driver` / `hatch_driver_window` design
- The front hatch is **very large** and gives the driver a good view of the ground in front.
- It is **pivoted/hinged open** (a large openable lid) with a **rubber seal** around it (compartment
  seals airtight under NBC) and a **long locking handle**.
- Over the windshield glass sits an **armored cover that opens & closes SEPARATELY**, worked by its
  own folded handle — i.e. a cover-over-the-window that articulates independently of the whole hatch.
  → This is exactly the two-hinge model we built: **whole lid (`hatch_driver`) + armored window cover
  (`hatch_driver_window`) + glass under it**.
- A **windshield wiper + motor** sit at the bottom edge of the glass; a **washer sprayer** and a
  **rain/sunshade/blast protector** are welded above the port.
- The same front-hatch periscope mount is reused for the **night periscope**.
- **Vision ports** on either side of the forward cab with **laminated glass blocks**, removable
  **covers**, washer + wiper; spare glass blocks stored in a white box on the hull.

## Driver's interior (for the "interior hint" if/when we add it)
- **Large instrument panel** dominates the LEFT side of the compartment.
- **Centrally located seat**, adjustable fore/aft, reclines; black cushions w/ white edging.
- **Gearshift**: simple **double-H pattern**; lever at the front of the seat; an **H-gate decal**
  on a box near the driver's feet (neutral centre, 5th+4th left, Reverse+1st right).
- Two **steering levers** (tiller bars) pushed fully forward when parked.
- **Pedals**: clutch / brake / accelerator, traditional positions; right steering lever to their left.
- **Battery shelf** under/around the panel; **compressed-air cylinder + compressor** for cold engine
  start (low, on the floor); **air tanks/controls** on the driver's left.
- **TVN-2** passive IR night periscope: **1× magnification, 30° FOV (H & V), good to ~30 m**; stows in
  a white box on the hull. FG-125 IR headlights provide the IR source.
- Soviet AFV tubing colour code (useful if we ever paint the interior): **blue = water**,
  **yellow = fuel/oil**, **red = fire-extinguisher**, **green = compressed air**; instrument
  boxes/gauges silver; floors/walls grey over brown primer.

## Rig takeaways
- Driver hatch two-hinge articulation is authentic — keep it.
- Driver is front-left → hatch placement side confirmed.
- If we add the interior hint: seat (centre, reclined) + the big left instrument panel + the H-gate
  shifter box are the readable hero elements.
