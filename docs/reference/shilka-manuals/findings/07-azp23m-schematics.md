# ЗСУ-23-4М — «Альбом рисунков и схем, Часть 1, АЗП-23М» (2011) — figure analysis

**Source:** `07-figures-schematics-part1-azp23m-2011.pdf` (42 pp.) — Целебровский А.И., Зарывалов Д.В., Гаврилов А.А.; Томский политехнический университет, 2011. A teaching figure-album (schemes, photos, drawings) of the **АЗП-23М** quad-23 mm automatic AA gun mount fitted to the **ЗСУ-23-4М «Шилка»**.

This is a FIGURES & SCHEMATICS album — every page is one labelled diagram/photo. Below: annotated figure list (page-by-page) followed by a synthesis on firing geometry and the laying-drive mechanisms.

## ⭐ Key numeric data — firing geometry & drives (pp. 4–5)

**Crew (p.4):** 1 Командир установки (commander), 2 Оператор поиска-наводчик (search operator/gunner), 3 Оператор дальности (range operator), 4 Механик-водитель (driver).

> **Modelling note on blast geometry:** the **slewing radius 2920 mm** (barrel-tip sweep circle at 0° elevation) and **line-of-fire height 2020 mm** (lower barrels above the horizon datum) directly bound where the four muzzles sit relative to the hull and where muzzle blast projects. At full depression (−4.5°) the lower-barrel muzzles are the limiting clearance case.

### p.5 — ⭐ УСТРОЙСТВО АЗП-23М (AZP-23M layout)

- **Основание с башней** — base + turret on the hull ring
Plus the main AZP-23M TTX table (firing-geometry values above). **This is the best single overview of how the gun mount sits in the turret** — two paired cradles forward, crew/avionics aft, armour shields on both flanks.

### p.6 — СОСТАВ АВТОМАТА (Autocannon composition)

### p.9 — ВЗАИМОДЕЙСТВИЕ ЧАСТЕЙ И МЕХАНИЗМОВ (Action cycle)
Storyboard of cutaway photos through the firing cycle: Досылание патрона в патронник (chambering) → Поднятие затвора (bolt rises/locks) → Положение подвижных частей при разбитии капсюля (primer strike) → Начало отпирания канала ствола (start of unlock) → Экстракция гильзы ×3 (case extraction) → Подача очередного патрона (next-round feed). The gas/short-recoil action sequence.

Good reference for the turret shell + how the gun structure mounts to the hull ring.

### p.11 — ⭐ УСТРОЙСТВО ЛЮЛЕК (Cradle design) — key elevation-geometry figure
Two rendered cradle assemblies + a travel-position photo. This is the **core of the elevation geometry**: the four guns are split into an **upper cradle (люлька верхняя)** and a **lower cradle (люлька нижняя)**, mechanically tied so all four barrels elevate together.
- **Upper cradle:** Корпус люльки верхней (upper body), **Цапфа** (trunnion), Задние крепления автоматов (rear gun mounts).
- **Lower cradle:** Кронштейн 3-й точки (3rd-point bracket), Труба (tube), **Труба гильзоотвода** (case-ejection tube), Хомут переднего крепления (front mount clamp), **Цапфа** (trunnion), Корпус люльки нижней (lower body), **Венец зубчатый** (toothed elevation arc/sector gear — this is what the elevation gearbox drives), **Противовес** (counterweight).
- **Тяга параллелограмма** (parallelogram linkage rod, photo) — the link that keeps the upper and lower cradles parallel so both pairs of barrels stay co-elevated.
- Inset: **«Заглушки сняты» / «Походное положение»** — muzzle plugs removed vs. travel position (barrels raised/clamped for transit).

### p.12 — МЕХАНИЗМ СВЯЗИ ЛЮЛЬКИ С ОПТИЧЕСКИМ ВИЗИРОМ (Cradle↔optical-sight link)
Photo of the bell-crank + push-rod linkage that mechanically transmits cradle elevation to the optical sight so the sight tracks the guns' elevation angle. (No callouts; shows the parallelogram arms and rod ends.)

### p.13 — ⭐ МЕХАНИЗМ ГОРИЗОНТАЛЬНОГО НАВЕДЕНИЯ (Traverse / azimuth drive)
Drawing + in-turret photo, callouts:
- **Маховик горизонтального наведения** — traverse handwheel (manual path)
- **Принимающий прибор** — receiver/servo instrument (takes the fire-control command)
- **Редуктор ГН** — traverse reduction gearbox
- **Гидромотор № 5** — **hydraulic motor No. 5** (the powered traverse drive)
→ **Traverse is hydraulically driven** (Гидромотор №5 → Редуктор ГН → погон pinion), with a manual handwheel fallback; the принимающий прибор feeds the auto/semi-auto laying commands. Unlimited 360° traverse (matches p.4/5).

### p.14 — ⭐ МЕХАНИЗМ ВЕРТИКАЛЬНОГО НАВЕДЕНИЯ (Elevation drive)
Three images:
- **Маховик вертикального наведения** — elevation handwheel (photo, at the gunner's station)
- **Редуктор вертикального наведения** — elevation gearbox (sectioned drawing)
- **Редуктор ручного наведения по вертикали** — manual-elevation gearbox (drawing)
→ Elevation has a powered gearbox (hydraulic, paired with the traverse hydraulics) **and** a separate manual handwheel/gearbox; output drives the **венец зубчатый** (toothed sector) on the lower cradle (p.11).

### p.15 — СТОПОР ГН + УПЛОТНЕНИЕ ПОГОНА (Traverse lock + turret-ring seal)
Photo, callouts: **Маховик горизонтального стопора** (traverse-lock handwheel) and **Вороток рукоятки устройства уплотнения погона** (lever for the turret-ring sealing device — seals the погон against weather/NBC). The traverse lock pins the turret for travel.

### p.16 — СТОПОР ВЕРТИКАЛЬНОГО НАВЕДЕНИЯ (Elevation lock)
Three photos: **Положение люльки «застопорено»** (cradle in the LOCKED state), **Стопор** (the lock pin/latch), **Паз люльки** (the slot in the cradle the lock engages). The elevation lock fixes the cradle at a set angle (travel) by dropping a pin into the cradle slot.

### p.17 — РУКОЯТКИ (Travel-lock & elevation-gearbox handles)
Photo of two grey ball-handles: **Рукоятка стопора походного положения по вертикали** (vertical travel-lock handle) and **Рукоятка редуктора вертикального наведения** (elevation-gearbox engage handle). Operator controls for locking elevation in travel and engaging the elevation gearbox.

### p.18 — КОМАНДИРСКАЯ БАШЕНКА (Commander's cupola)
Photo: **КПН (походное положение)** — commander's pointer/day device in travel position; **ТПКУ-2** — commander's combined periscope sight. Open hatch visible.

### p.19 — ЛИМБ И НОНИУС КОМАНДИРСКОЙ БАШЕНКИ (Cupola scale + vernier)

### p.21 — МЕХАНИЗМ ПОВОРОТА БАШЕНКИ И ПРИБОР БМО-190 (Cupola rotation + BMO-190 device)
Photo, callouts: **Прибор наблюдения БМО-190** (commander's observation device) and **Механизм поворота башенки** (the cupola-rotation drum/gear column). Also visible in-frame: the red warning plate «ЗАПРЕЩАЕТСЯ ПИРОЗАРЯЖАНИЕ ПРИ ОТКАЗЕ АВТОМАТОВ ВО ВРЕМЯ СТРЕЛЬБЫ» (no pyro-charging on gun stoppage while firing) and the round-counter panels (ЗАРЯЖЕНО / ОСТАТОК ПАТРОНОВ).

### p.22 — СИСТЕМА ПИТАНИЯ АВТОМАТОВ (Ammo feed system) — belt + box

### p.35 — ⭐ ПУЛЬТ КОМАНДИРА legend (firing & laying controls)
38 items. The firing-geometry / laying-relevant ones:
- **17 – переключатель «ОГРАНИЧЕНИЕ УГЛОВ» (ПОУ)** — *angle-limit selector* (the firing-zone / forbidden-arc limiter that protects the host hull/own structure — see synthesis).

## ⭐ SYNTHESIS — firing geometry & laying drives (modelling-critical)

### Elevation / traverse envelope
- **Elevation:** **−4.5° to +85.5°** (catalogue value; the gun-data table gives the tolerance form **−4°30′ … +85°30′**). At full depression −4.5° the **lower** barrels are the clearance-limiting pair.
- **Traverse:** **unlimited / full 360°** (continuous, hydraulic). No mechanical traverse end-stops; the only arc restriction is the electrically-selectable **«ОГРАНИЧЕНИЕ УГЛОВ» / ПОУ** angle limiter on the commander's panel (p.35 item 17) — i.e. a **software/relay forbidden-zone** limiter, not a hard cam. (This album shows no «зона обстрела» drawing and no mechanical traverse copir/cam — the forbidden arc is handled electrically.)
- **Slewing radius 2920 mm** (barrel-tip sweep at 0° elevation) and **line-of-fire height 2020 mm** (lower barrels above horizon) fix where the muzzles sit over the hull and where blast projects. Vehicle envelope L 6495 / W 3075 / H 3765 mm.

### Gun arrangement & elevation geometry (the cradle system — pp.5, 11)
- Four **2А7 23 mm** autocannons in **two cradles**: **upper cradle (люлька верхняя)** carries the two upper guns, **lower cradle (люлька нижняя)** the two lower guns.
- The cradles share **trunnions (цапфы)** and are tied by a **parallelogram linkage (тяга параллелограмма)** so all four barrels stay parallel and elevate as one unit.
- Elevation is driven onto the **toothed sector/arc (венец зубчатый)** on the lower cradle; a **counterweight (противовес)** balances the mass about the trunnions. The same cradle motion is mechanically fed to the optical sight via the **cradle↔sight link (p.12)** and to the commander's **ТПКУ-2** sight (its own VN mechanism, p.20).
- Travel position: barrels raised and clamped, **muzzle plugs (заглушки)** fitted (p.11 inset).

### Laying drives — powered vs manual (pp.13, 14, 31)
| Axis | Powered path | Manual path | Lock(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Traverse (ГН)** | **Гидромотор №5 → Редуктор ГН → погон** ring pinion; fed by **Принимающий прибор** (servo receiver from fire-control) | **Маховик горизонтального наведения** (handwheel) | **Стопор ГН** + turret-ring seal (p.15) |
| **Elevation (ВН)** | hydraulic **Редуктор ВН** (engaged via Рукоятка редуктора ВН, p.17) → венец зубчатый on lower cradle | **Маховик вертикального наведения** + **Редуктор ручного наведения по вертикали** | **Стопор ВН** (pin into Паз люльки, p.16) + travel-lock (p.17) |
- The whole powered system is the **АБУ** automatic gun-laying unit driving through the **Редуктор в сборе с АБУ** (p.31); the hydraulic drive is switched by «гидропривод вкл/выкл» (panel 14–16).
- **Speeds (from p.5):** auto — az 70°/s, el 60°/s; semi-auto — az 70°/s, el 65±10°/s; accel — az 55°/s², el 50°/s²; **manual — 15±5°/s both axes**.

### Firing control & interlocks (pp.35–37)
- Four guns fire as **two electrically-separable banks** — *upper pair* and *lower pair* — through electric triggers **ЭЛСП I–IV** (p.37), mirroring the two-cradle split. Triggered by **fire grip button** or **firing pedal** (p.36), per-gun round counters СП1–4.
- **Fire is interlocked** on: hatch closed («ЛЮК»), cooling running («ОХЛ/Охлаждение»), coolant level OK («Уровень ОЖ»), auto-laying ready («АБУ»), fire-control enable (СРП), interlock-check OK («КОНТРОЛЬ БЛОКИРОВОК»), with a **0.3 s delay relay (РВ2)**. **«АВАРИЙНАЯ СТРЕЛЬБА»** bypasses interlocks for emergency fire.
- **Barrel cooling** is a closed liquid loop (pump Д-4500 motor, ~8 atm) around all four barrels, steam-vented (pp.28–29) — must be running to fire.

> **Note for the «MITRI»/Shilka game model:** unlimited 360° traverse with no hard end-stops; elevation hard-limited **−4.5°…+85.5°**; the only "forbidden zone" is an electrically-selectable angle limiter (ПОУ), not a physical cam — so a forbidden-arc behaviour should be implemented as a configurable software sector, not a geometric stop. The four muzzles sweep a 2920 mm-radius circle at 2020 mm height; that is where muzzle blast/flash originates relative to the hull.
