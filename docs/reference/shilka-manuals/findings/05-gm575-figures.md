## 0. ALBUM STRUCTURE (Перечень рисунков, pp.3-7)

The GM-575 chassis is built around a **В-6** family diesel (6-cylinder, the inline-6 half of the V-2/В-12 V-engine), a **mechanical gearbox + двухступенчатые планетарные механизмы поворота (ПМП = two-stage planetary steering mechanisms)** transmission, and torsion-bar suspension. There is also a **gas-turbine reference** ("К турбине") in the fuel system — the ДГ4М-1 turbine-generator APU.

## 1. POWER PLANT — fuel system figures (Гл.1, pp.8-20 read)

**Engine identity:** the fuel pump is **НК-6** (fig 1-16) — a six-plunger in-line HP injection pump = **6-cylinder** engine → confirms the **В-6** diesel (the inline-six derived from the V-2/В-2 family). Air-start system (figs 1-56…1-59) means the engine has **compressed-air starting** in addition to the electric СТ-721 starter — dual start.

- **RIS 1-1** (p8): hand inserting the **регляж (gauge)** through the cylinder-head-cover hatch to find cyl-1 TDC. Callouts: 1-регляж; 2-люк крышки головки блока; 3-крышка головки блока.
- **RIS 1-2** (p8): camshaft timing — intake & exhaust cam lobes each shown at **38°** ("Вид со стороны передачи" = view from transmission end). Labels клапан впуска / клапан выпуска.
- **RIS 1-3** (p8): **Моментоскоп** injection-timing tool: 1-glass tube, 2-rubber tube, 3-metal tube, 4-union nut, 5-pump fitting.
- **RIS 1-4** (p9) ⭐ **СХЕМА СИСТЕМЫ ПИТАНИЯ ТОПЛИВОМ** — whole fuel system. Flow legend: работа двигателя / работа подогревателя / прокачка двигателя / выпуск воздуха из насоса / забор топлива в бачок гидропневмоаккумулятора. Note **"К турбине"** (to turbine) branches → fuel also feeds the gas-turbine APU. Callouts: 1-гидропневмоаккумулятор; 2-топливозакачивающий насос МЗН-2; 3-кран выпуска воздуха; 4-манометр; 5-пробка выпуска воздуха из бачка; 6-кран слива топлива; 7-клапан; 8-топливораспределительный кран; 9,12-приёмные трубки с сетчатыми фильтрами; 10-передний бак; 11-задний бак; 13-дроссель; 14-топливный насос; 15,17-фильтры тонкой очистки; 16-топливоподкачивающий насос БНК-12ТК; 18-подогреватель; 19,20-электромагнитные клапаны; 21-фильтр грубой очистки. Two tanks: front + rear.
- **RIS 1-5/1-6** (p10): solenoid valve МКТ-17М (sectioned, Вход/Выход), and tank drain valve (ball + spring).
- **RIS 1-7/1-8** (pp.11): front & rear fuel-tank mounting in the hull (brackets, гасket strips, чехол cover, filler neck + hose clamps for rear).
- **RIS 1-9** (p12): tank filler cap — двойной (outer/inner body) with выпускной + впускной (relief + vacuum) valves & springs, atmosphere vent hole "а".
- **RIS 1-10** (p12): coarse fuel filter — corrugated-cup gauze element, Вход/Выход.
- **RIS 1-11** (p13): **БНК-12ТК** booster pump — rotary vane pump (ротор 16, плавающий палец 17, лопасти blades), bypass + reduction valves, rotation-direction & flow-path arrows.
- **RIS 1-12/1-13** (p14): fine fuel filter (felt/silk or kapron sleeve element 16-17, metal mesh 18) + its cover with air-bleed fitting.
- **RIS 1-14** (p15): **гидропневмоаккумулятор** (hydro-pneumatic accumulator) cylinder w/ electric МЗН-2 priming pump 8, electric motor 10, fuel cocks. Used to pre-prime/pressurise fuel before start. "На прокачку и к подогревателю".

### Гл.1 cont. (pp.21-39 read) — injector, throttle linkage, lube & cooling
- **RIS 1-20** (p21): **форсунка (injector)** — closed-type, needle valve (игла распылителя 2), nozzle hole, "Цифры указывают группу гидравлического сопротивления" (numbers = hydraulic-resistance group). Slot filter (щелевой фильтр) shown.

## 2. ⭐⭐ SILOVAYA PEREDACHA — THE DRIVETRAIN (Гл.2, fig 2-1 read; pp.58+)

### RIS 2-1 (p58, Вклейка 6) — КИНЕМАТИЧЕСКАЯ СХЕМА СИЛОВОЙ ПЕРЕДАЧИ — the master driveline diagram
**The single most important figure for driving.** Power flow, left-to-right callouts:
1. **коленчатый вал двигателя** (engine crankshaft) →
2. **маховик** (flywheel) →
6. **редуктор силовой передачи** (transmission reduction/transfer gearbox) — at the reduktor the power **splits**: a branch via **3-фрикционная муфта СЭП + 4-механизм включения шестерни отбора мощности** runs **"На привод СЭП (системы первичного электропитания)"** = the **power-take-off that drives the primary electrical-power generator** for the radar/turret. (This is why the Shilka can't traverse/track with the engine off without the СЭП.)
7. **главный фрикцион** (main clutch / master friction clutch) — on the drive line to the gearbox →
5,8. **соединительные валы** (connecting shafts) →
10. **коробка передач** (gearbox) →
9,11. **правая и левая полуоси** (right & left cross/half-shafts, gearbox output to each side) →
12. **планетарный механизм поворота** (planetary steering mechanism = ПМП, **one per side**, with band brakes) →
13. **бортовая передача** (final drive, one per side) →
14. **ведущее колесо** (drive sprocket) → track.

**Driveline summary (game model):** engine → flywheel → transfer gearbox (taps off the radar/turret generator) → **main clutch** → **multi-speed gearbox** → splits to **two ПМП planetary steering units** (one per track) → two **final drives** → two **front drive sprockets**. Steering is **clutch-and-brake via the planetary ПМП**, not a differential — pulling a tiller disengages/brakes that side's track to turn. Drive sprockets are at the **front**.

### ⭐⭐ ПМП STEERING + BRAKES + FINAL DRIVE (pp.76-84) — how the Shilka turns
- **RIS 2-22** (p76) gearbox→main-clutch coupling (соединит. вал 11 + toothed couplings 6/9 + spring 12). RIS 2-23/2-24 = assembly (silk-thread sealant between case halves, bolt-torque order).
- **RIS 2-25** (p77) ⭐⭐ **ПЛАНЕТАРНЫЙ МЕХАНИЗМ ПОВОРОТА (ПРАВЫЙ)** = the **2-stage planetary steering mechanism (ПМП), one per track.** Key elements: **15-солнечная шестерня (sun gear), 39-сателлит (planet gear) on 43-ось/12-водило (carrier, "зубчатый барабан с ободом"), 13-коронная шестерня (ring/crown gear); 18-26 блокировочный фрикцион (multi-disc LOCKING clutch — discs 19 external-tooth / 20 internal-tooth, drum 21); 11-барабан остановочного тормоза (STOPPING-brake drum); 31-ведущий вал бортовой передачи (output → final drive).** 
  **How it steers (classic Soviet 2-stage ПМП):**
  - **Both lock-clutches engaged, no brakes** → planetary sets rotate solid 1:1 → **both tracks full speed = straight ahead.**
  - **Release one side's lock-clutch** → that side's planetary set goes to its **reduced ratio** → that track slows → **gentle turn** (tiller position I).
  - **Release lock-clutch + apply that side's stopping brake** (brake the sun gear) → that track **stops** → **sharp pivot turn** (tiller position II). Brake both = vehicle stop / hold.
- **RIS 2-26** (p78) plug layout + oil level on the ПМП drum cover (control plug #4 item 10, drain plug 41).
- **RIS 2-27** (p79) ⭐ **ТОРМОЗ** — the band brakes. **Two brakes are distinguished: «блокировочный тормоз» (locking) and «остановочный тормоз» (stopping)** plus their linkage: тормозные колодки/ленты 2/11/34, двуплечий рычаг тормоза 20, тормозная тяга 26, **тяга блокировочного тормоза 28, тяга остановочного тормоза 29**.
- **RIS 2-28** (p80) **СХЕМА РАБОТЫ ТОРМОЗА** — the band brake is **self-energizing/servo** (P = applied force, T = friction); wrap direction differs «при левом вращении барабана» vs «при правом вращении» — i.e. brake assist depends on track rotation direction.
- **RIS 2-29** (p81) ⭐ **РЫЧАГ УПРАВЛЕНИЯ (ЛЕВЫЙ)** = the **left steering tiller**. рукоятка 1 (grip), пружина 7, **сектор 8 (detent quadrant), ролик 11** that snaps into sector notches (positions **Н/У/В**, 0.5 mm clearance) — this gives the tiller its **two detented pull stages**. Note **16 = кнопка включения насоса стеклообмыва (windscreen-wash pump button built into the tiller grip).**
- **RIS 2-30** (p82) tiller installation (shaft 5, sector 8, base 9, trumpet-tube 16).
- **RIS 2-31** (p83, Вклейка 9) ⭐⭐ **ПРИВОД УПРАВЛЕНИЯ ПМП И ТОРМОЗАМИ** — the master driver-controls linkage. Names the **pedals & levers**: **4 = педаль горного тормоза (mountain/parking-brake pedal)**, 3-гребёнка + 5-планка с зубом (its ratchet hold), **6 = рычаг с рукояткой (the tiller)**, трубы валов 7/14/55, rod chains 11-51 to each ПМП. **Вид А also shows «Педаль главного фрикциона» (clutch pedal)** for spatial reference. So the floor has **3 pedals: clutch + accelerator + mountain/parking brake** (plus 2 tillers, gear lever, PTO lever).
- **RIS 2-32** (p83) left brake-control bracket (35 mm lever dimension).
- **RIS 2-33** (p84) ⭐⭐ **ПОЛОЖЕНИЯ РЫЧАГОВ УПРАВЛЕНИЯ** — hand-photos of the tiller positions (the actual *driving* gestures):
  - **а)** исходное — levers fully **forward** = straight, drive engaged.
  - **б)** **I положение** — both levers pulled **fully back** = both lock-clutches released (declutch / coast both tracks).
  - **в)** transition to **II** — grips **rotated inward**.
  - **г)** **II положение** — grips rotated **AND** pulled fully back = both **stopping brakes applied** (full brake / pivot).
  - → **Game steering model:** pull one tiller part-way = soft turn that side; pull it with grip twisted = hard pivot/brake that side; pull both = neutral; both twisted+back = stop/park.

**КОРОБКА ПЕРЕДАЧ / gearbox** (figs 2-8…2-19) — **5 forward + 1 reverse**, 3 shafts (input/intermediate/main), **synchros only on 2-3 and 4-5** (1st & reverse are sliding-mesh dog clutches — can grind), **H-gate pattern (figs 2-19, 2-21): V-III-Reverse top row, IV-II-I bottom row**, remote linkage (figs 2-20/2-21), interlock so only one gear at a time (fig 2-18), speedometer driven off the output (fig 2-16) →
output splits L/R to **two ДВУХСТУПЕНЧАТЫЕ ПЛАНЕТАРНЫЕ МЕХАНИЗМЫ ПОВОРОТА / 2-stage planetary steering mechanisms (ПМП), one per track** (fig 2-25): each = a planetary set (sun/planets/ring) + a **multi-disc locking clutch (блокировочный фрикцион)** + a **band stopping-brake (остановочный тормоз)** (brake figs 2-27/2-28, self-energizing) →
**БОРТОВАЯ ПЕРЕДАЧА / single-stage final drive, one per side** (fig 2-34) →
**front DRIVE SPROCKET** (2 toothed rings, fig 3-2) → **RMSh track** (fig 3-1).
**Steering logic (figs 2-25, 2-29, 2-33):** straight = both lock-clutches engaged. **Tiller pulled part-way** (position I) disengages that side's lock-clutch → that planetary set drops to its reduced ratio → that track slows → **gentle turn**. **Tiller pulled with grip rotated inward** (position II) also applies that side's stopping brake → that track stops → **sharp pivot**. Pull both = declutch both; both + rotate = full brake/park. Separate **mountain/parking-brake pedal** (fig 2-31, item 4) with a ratchet hold.
**Suspension (figs 3-3…3-11):** **6 rubber-tyred road wheels per side** on balancers, sprung by **6 transverse torsion bars per side** (colour-coded **yellow = left, blue = right**, fig 3-11), with **rotary-vane hydraulic dampers** on the end stations (figs 3-9/3-10; 4 damper niches, fig 5-2). Front drive sprocket, rear **idler on a tensioning crank** (figs 3-4/3-5, tensioned by the screw tool fig 9-8). Engine **starts two ways: electric СТ-721 starter (figs 4-4/4-7) OR compressed-air bottle (fig 1-56)**; cold-start heater (figs 1-49…1-54). Cooling is **exhaust-ejector draught** through the radiators (figs 1-44/1-45) with **powered louvers (МПК-5А, fig 1-48)** — no engine fan.
