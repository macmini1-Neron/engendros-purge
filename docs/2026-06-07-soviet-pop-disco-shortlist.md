## Sourcing playbook (verified)

1. **Internet Archive `archive.org/details/70s_hits`** — 100-track *Хит-парад 70-х*, VBR-MP3 (~647 MB) + OGG + torrent. **Confirmed to contain** Земляне «Трава у дома» (#29), Самоцветы «Мой адрес» (#50), Ялла «Учкудук», Пугачёва «Арлекино», Леонтьев «Зелёный свет». Single best bulk source for picks 1–4, 7, 20.
2. **Internet Archive `archive.org/details/sofiarotaru`** — 33 Rotaru files (OGG/MP3/torrent); confirmed «Луна-луна». Covers picks 6, 13.
3. **`sovetskaya-estrada.ru`** — per-artist catalogs, counts verified: Ротару 80 · Пугачёва 90 · Леонтьев 78 · Магомаев 64 · Антонов 44 · Весёлые ребята 34 · Самоцветы 33 · Земляне 16. Good fallback for picks 5, 8, 15, 19.
4. **YouTube** (yt-dlp → mp3, then normalize to 128 k stereo like the last batch) — for the synth-pop/rock picks (Мираж, Электроклуб, Форум, Браво, Ласковый май, Zodiac, Секрет) that the archives don't carry. Prefer official-label/Мелодия uploads or full-length album rips; **avoid truncated lyric-video edits** (the bug that bit `komarovo`/`katyusha` — see `engendros-jukebox-truncated-songs-fix`).
5. **AVOID** `sovmusic.ru` for this batch — verified to be patriotic/march-only, **no estrada/disco section** (single "эстрада" hit on the site is an orchestra credit, not a genre). And ignore "Sovietwave/synthwave" comps and the Spotify "диско 70-80" album — those are *modern* remakes / Western disco with Russian titles, not authentic recordings.
