# P-37 «Bar Lock» — per-form analysis (from the Lešany walkaround w01–w36)

Compiled by reading the walkaround images directly (the research subagent downloaded all 36
+ wNet1 but crashed before writing this file). Observations are **observed from photos**, not
measured. w01 = TTX placard, w02 = hero 3/4, w05 = rear, w08/w10/w11 = other museum exhibits
(Ural truck / S-75 missiles / R-17) — NOT the P-37.

## 1. The two reflectors & their arrangement (the key fix)
- **Two open-lattice parabolic-trough reflectors, clearly SEPARATED — they do NOT overlap.** (w02, w03, w13)
- **UPPER**: larger, mounted HIGH and BACK on the central frame, tilted back hard (~45–55° from
  vertical → face points well up). (w02, w03)
- **LOWER**: a touch smaller, mounted LOW and FORWARD (near cabin-roof height), tilted back only
  gently (~15–20°). (w02, w13)
- The two fan apart like an open book; the **feed column sits in the throat between them**. The gap
  is made by the upper being high+back and the lower low+front (Z offset), not by stacking.
- **Outline = top-clipped pentagon**: bottom edge full width, vertical sides, the **top two corners
  cut ~45°** to a narrower top edge. Bottom corners are square. (w04, w06, w12)
- Surface = **fine square open mesh** of flat slats with bolt bumps at the crossings; see-through.
  ~8–9 vertical ribs, many horizontal slats. (w12 rear, w16, w14)

## 2. Central support structure
- A tall **rectangular riveted box-frame mast with X cross-bracing** rises from the cabin roof and
  carries both reflectors; this is the rotating spine. Riveted sheet panels + tubular braces. (w12, w20, w24)

## 3. Feed / illuminators (блок облучателей)
- A **vertical feed column in front of the reflector at the focal line, carrying ~5–6 round
  horn/illuminator cups** stacked vertically (the stacked-beam feed). (w13)
- Individual feeds are **waveguide-fed horns** (curved waveguide → horn aperture); horn apertures
  read as round/square cups on plates. (w14, w16)

## 4. Cabin (636А)
- Riveted sheet-metal box, **panel seams + bolt rows**, at least one **window** (barred) and access
  **doors**; **waveguide pipes/conduits run up the cabin sides** to the antennas. Soviet green. (w24, w33, w20)

## 5. Trailer (52-У-415М) & rotating base
- **2 axles**, twin road wheels; **leaf-spring suspension**. (w12, w29, w33)
- **A-frame drawbar** at the front with a **towing eye/lunette** + a **central screw jack stand**. (w12, w33)
- **4 outrigger/levelling jacks** deployed wide at the corners. (w12)
- **Tail lights** (red) on the rear cross-member; an **azimuth drive motor** sits by the turntable;
  the cabin rotates on a **turntable** on the trailer bed. (w33)

## 6. Livery
- Cabin/trailer Soviet **olive green**; reflector lattice bare **grey galvanised steel**. (w02, w03, all)
